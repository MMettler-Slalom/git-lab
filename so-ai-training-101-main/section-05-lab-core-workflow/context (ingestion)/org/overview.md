# Mary's Place — Organizational Overview

> **Audience:** Product team building software for Mary's Place.

---

## Who They Are

- Nonprofit based in Seattle, serving King County
- Operates family shelters and housing-stability programs
- Primary focus: ensuring no child sleeps outside

---

## Vision

- A community where **all families have safety, stability, and housing**

---

## Mission

- Ensure **no child sleeps outside**
- Center equity and opportunity for women and families
- Deliver a continuum of services: emergency shelter, mobile outreach, diversion, and housing supports that help families regain stable housing

---

## Core Values

| Value | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Love** | Every guest is welcomed, respected, accepted, and cared for |
| **Collaboration** | Partner regionally; elevate family voices |
| **Equity** | Inclusive environments; equitable access and opportunity for all |
| **Stewardship** | Careful stewardship of financial, material, human, and story-based resources |
| **Accountability** | Board, staff, volunteers, and guests held to commitments |
| **Responsiveness** | Address urgent needs with creativity, adaptability, and persistence |

---

## Organizational Goals

- Move families quickly into safe, stable housing
- Expand access and operational efficiency across shelters and programs
- Reduce manual processes (e.g., bed-availability tracking)
- Improve real-time resource visibility across sites
- Maximize social impact
- Prototype scalable solutions that can extend beyond a single agency
