# Interview Notes — Busy Parent

**Participant:** "Marcus T." (pseudonym), 38, dad of 3 (ages 2, 6, 11)
**Date:** May 13, 2026, 8:15–9:10 PM (rescheduled twice — finally did it after kids' bedtime)
**Interviewer:** M. Mettler
**Format:** Phone call (participant on couch, "don't make me turn on video pls")

---

## Background

- Spouse works nights (ER nurse), so Marcus handles ~80% of kids' healthcare admin
- Aging dad in another state — sometimes coordinates that too
- Providers: pediatrician (all 3 kids), his PCP, derm for oldest kid (eczema), OT for middle kid, dentist x2, his own dentist + eye doc
- "Probably 15 appts a year across the family. At least."
- Device: iPhone 99% of the time. Laptop "exists but I never open it"
- Time per task: "give me 90 seconds or I'll call you, sorry"

## Current pain points

- Last task: refill on middle kid's ADHD med. Took 4 days, 2 portal messages, 1 phone call. "Controlled substance thing I get it but the process is opaque"
- Most annoying: **separate logins for each kid**. "I have a Notes app full of usernames"
- Phone calls when: anything controlled substance, anything urgent, anything involving more than one kid at once
- Missed: dentist appt for oldest in Feb. Reminder went to spouse's email (she had originally booked it). Got charged no-show fee
- "Spouse and I are not the same person, why does the system think we are sometimes and not other times"

## Family / proxy management — **biggest pain**

- WANTS: single login, dropdown to switch between family members, badge counts per kid
- Wants spouse to have equal access — "co-equal parent mode" not "I delegated to her"
- Age transition: "11yo is going to be 13 soon, I know there's some teen privacy thing coming. I have no idea how that works and I'm anxious about it"
- Adolescent privacy: surprised by it, wants warning + clear explanation, not surprise lockout
- Grandparents: occasionally takes kids to grandparent's house. Would love temp view-only access for immunization records (school/camp forms) — not full record
- Babysitter: "honestly just give me a PDF of allergies + emergency info I can text"
- Most-needed-on-short-notice: **immunization records** (camp/school deadlines!), allergies, current meds, insurance card image

## Appointments

- Wants real-time booking. Hates "request and wait" — "if I have to wait for callback I'll just call"
- Stacking: YES. "If I could book all 3 kids' annual physicals in one Saturday morning slot, life-changing"
- Sick visits: needs same-day. Wants to see "next available" across multiple providers in practice, not just "their" pediatrician
- Reminders: 24hr before = useful. 1 week before = ignored. Day-of-morning = panic-mode helpful
- DOES NOT WANT: marketing emails disguised as reminders. "If I see one 'flu shots now available!' email I'll uninstall"

## Messaging

- Pediatric question: today, takes a photo, texts spouse, googles, then maybe portal-messages doctor 2 days later when not better
- Async nurse chat: **YES. Big yes.** "Even a 'this looks normal, watch for X, call if Y' would be huge"
- Photo/video: critical for rashes, eyes, etc. Concern: where do those photos live? Are they secure? Can he delete?
- Response time: 4 hrs non-urgent, 30 min "worried"
- "If it's actually urgent I'm calling 911 or going to ER, I'm not waiting for a portal message"

## Mobile experience

- App, not web. "If it's a web wrapper I'll know within 5 seconds and uninstall"
- Must work on mobile: messaging, scheduling, refills, immunization records, ID cards, bill pay
- Notifications: appt reminders YES, message replies YES, refill ready YES. Marketing/health tips/newsletters HARD NO
- Liked app: One Medical (used to use before insurance changed). "Felt like a normal modern app, not government software"

## Billing / admin

- Bills are "deliberately confusing." Gets paper bills + portal bills + EOBs that don't match. Has paid wrong bill twice
- Wants: ALL family bills in one place. Pay from app. Apple Pay/Google Pay
- HSA: tracks receipts in a separate app (Lively). Would love one-click "this is HSA eligible" tagging
- Payment plans: yes, but transparent. "Don't bury fees"

## Telehealth

- Used 2x for self (sinus infection, follow-up). Liked it
- For kids: pediatrician offered for rashes — "honestly worked great, didn't have to load 3 kids in the car for 5min visit"
- No-go: anything where doc needs to actually look in ears/throat, anything emotional/mental health for the kids

## Trust / privacy

- Trusts the health system "about as much as anything else, which is not a lot but ok"
- Kid privacy: hopes school nurses can't see everything. Wants to know who's accessed records
- Reassurance: clear audit log, easy way to revoke access, MFA

## Wrap-up

- "Tuesday evening different" = doesn't have to make any phone calls during dinner because something got missed
- Magic wand: **one app, one login, all my kids, fast, doesn't email me garbage**
- Missed: asked about insurance card storage — "I take a photo of the card every Jan and I'd love to just have it in the app"

---

## Researcher observations

- Time-poor, impatient with friction. Will abandon flows >3 taps quickly
- Strong negative reactions to: separate logins per family member, marketing emails, "request a callback" flows
- Strong positive cues: stacked scheduling, async nurse, photo messaging
- Spouse-parity model is a real product requirement, not just a nice-to-have for this segment
