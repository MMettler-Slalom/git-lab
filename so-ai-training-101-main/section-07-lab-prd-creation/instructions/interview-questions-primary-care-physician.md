# Interview Questions: Primary Care Physician (Provider)

**Persona Snapshot:** Sees a high volume of patients across a broad scope. Time-pressured, juggles inbox messages, chart review, e-prescribing, and documentation. Wants tools that reduce clicks and context switching, not add to them.

**Session Length:** ~60 minutes
**Format:** Respect the clock — be ready to compress sections. Concrete, workflow-focused questions land better than abstract ones.

---

## 1. Warm-Up & Practice Context (5 min)

- Tell me about your panel — size, patient mix, typical day structure.
- How is your day split between visits, inbox/admin, and documentation?
- What systems do you touch in a typical day (EHR, portal, e-prescribe, fax, phone)?
- What does a "good day" vs. a "bad day" look like in terms of administrative load?

## 2. Pain Points & Burnout Drivers (10 min)

- What part of your administrative workload do you most resent?
- Where do you find yourself doing duplicate work across systems?
- What do you do at home in the evenings ("pajama time") that you wish you didn't?
- Tell me about the last time a tool actually saved you time. What made it work?
- What's a recent moment where the portal or EHR made you angry?

## 3. Inbox & Patient Messaging (10 min)

- Walk me through how you triage your inbox today.
- How do you decide what to handle yourself vs. delegate to staff?
- What's a reasonable patient message volume per day before it becomes unmanageable?
- How should asynchronous patient messages be compensated or scoped (clinical vs. admin)?
- What kinds of messages should *never* reach your inbox? How should they be routed?
- How would you want AI assistance (drafting, summarizing, classifying) to show up — and where should it stop?

## 4. Chart Review & Clinical Context (8 min)

- When opening a patient chart, what are the first 3–5 things you look for?
- How long do you ideally want to spend prepping for a visit?
- What information is hardest to find quickly today?
- What would a great "patient at a glance" summary contain?
- How important is it to see data from outside specialists, ERs, or wearables?

## 5. Care Team Collaboration (7 min)

- How do you communicate with nurses, MAs, and specialists today? What's broken?
- How do you currently make and track referrals? Where does it fall down?
- What would meaningful provider-to-provider messaging look like — and how do you avoid it becoming another inbox?
- How do you handle handoffs (coverage, vacation, after-hours)?

## 6. Prescribing & Refills (5 min)

- Walk me through a typical refill request workflow.
- What guardrails do you want around patient-initiated refill requests?
- What controlled substance / DEA / EPCS workflows matter most?
- How do you want medication reconciliation to work?

## 7. Telehealth & Hybrid Care (5 min)

- For which visit types is telehealth a clear win vs. a poor fit?
- What technical issues come up most in video visits today?
- How do you want to handle scheduling, documentation, and follow-up for virtual visits?

## 8. Performance, Reliability & Trust (4 min)

- What's your tolerance for portal downtime during clinic hours?
- How fast must a chart, message, or order screen open to feel acceptable?
- How do you want to be alerted to system outages or degraded performance?

## 9. Data, Insights & Population Health (3 min)

- What patient-level alerts actually change your behavior vs. become noise?
- What population-level views would help your panel (gaps in care, risk stratification)?
- How do you want quality metric performance surfaced?

## 10. Wrap-Up & Vision (3 min)

- If you could remove one task entirely from your day, what would it be?
- What would make you a vocal advocate for this portal among your peers?
- What didn't I ask that we'd regret missing?
