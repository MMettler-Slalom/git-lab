# Interview Questions: Busy Parent (Patient, 30–45)

**Persona Snapshot:** Manages healthcare for themselves and one or more dependents. Values speed, mobile convenience, and the ability to handle family care in small windows of time.

**Session Length:** ~60 minutes
**Format:** Semi-structured; expect interruptions — keep questions focused.

---

## 1. Warm-Up & Family Context (5 min)

- Tell me about who you're managing healthcare for — yourself, kids, partner, aging parents?
- How many providers are involved across your household in a typical year?
- What device do you most often use for healthcare tasks, and where are you when you use it?
- How much time, realistically, do you have at once to deal with healthcare admin?

## 2. Current Experience & Pain Points (10 min)

- Walk me through the last healthcare-related task you did for a family member.
- What's the single most annoying thing about managing your family's healthcare today?
- Where do you end up using phone calls instead of digital tools? Why?
- How do you currently juggle separate logins or accounts for each family member?
- Have you ever missed something important (appointment, refill, result) because of how info was delivered?

## 3. Family & Proxy Account Management (12 min)

- How do you want to switch between your kids' records and your own?
- At what age do you expect a child's record to become more private from you? How should that transition work?
- Do you share healthcare duties with a co-parent or partner? How would shared access work ideally?
- Would you want grandparents or babysitters to have limited access (e.g., view immunization records)?
- What information about your kids do you most often need on short notice (immunizations, allergies, meds)?

## 4. Appointment Scheduling (10 min)

- When you need to book an appointment, what's your first move?
- How important is seeing real-time availability vs. requesting a slot?
- Would you book multiple family members back-to-back if you could? What would make that work?
- How do you handle pediatric sick visits — same-day urgency vs. routine?
- What reminders do you actually find useful vs. ignore?

## 5. Messaging & Quick Questions (7 min)

- For a quick pediatric question ("is this rash normal?"), what would you ideally do?
- How would you feel about an asynchronous nurse triage chat?
- Would photo or video attachments be useful in messages? Any concerns?
- What response time feels acceptable for non-urgent vs. "I'm worried" questions?

## 6. Mobile Experience (8 min)

- Would you install a dedicated app, or do you prefer mobile web? Why?
- What features absolutely *must* work on mobile for it to be worth installing?
- How important are push notifications vs. email vs. text? Any that you'd turn off immediately?
- Have you used a healthcare app you actually liked? What made it work?

## 7. Billing & Administrative Tasks (4 min)

- How do you handle medical bills today? Where does it get confusing?
- Would you want to pay bills from the app? Set up payment plans?
- How do you keep track of HSA/FSA receipts and reimbursements?

## 8. Telehealth (3 min)

- Have you used a video visit for yourself or a child? How did it go?
- For which situations would telehealth be a "yes" vs. "no" for your family?

## 9. Trust, Privacy & Wrap-Up (3 min)

- Any concerns about your kids' health info being in a portal? What would reassure you?
- If we got this right, what would your Tuesday evening look like differently?
- What didn't I ask that I should have?
