# Interview Questions: Specialist (Provider)

**Persona Snapshot:** Provides episodic, focused care often initiated by a referral. Needs context from PCPs and other specialists, manages procedures, and coordinates complex cases. Visibility into the full patient care journey is often lacking.

**Session Length:** ~60 minutes
**Format:** Focus on referral flow, cross-provider coordination, and procedural workflows that differ from PCP needs.

---

## 1. Warm-Up & Practice Context (5 min)

- What's your specialty and typical case mix?
- What percentage of your patients come via referral vs. self-referred vs. internal?
- How is your week structured — clinic, procedures, on-call, consults?
- What systems do you and your staff touch in a given day?

## 2. Referral Intake & Triage (12 min)

- Walk me through what happens when a referral arrives.
- What information *must* be in a referral for it to be actionable? What's usually missing?
- How do you triage urgency on incoming referrals?
- What's the typical lag between referral received and patient seen? Where does it get stuck?
- How do you communicate back to the referring provider — and how often does that loop actually close?
- How would you want incomplete referrals to be handled (auto-reject, request more info, escalate)?

## 3. Pre-Visit Preparation (8 min)

- When prepping for a new consult, what do you most need that's hardest to get?
- How do you handle outside records (imaging, labs, prior op notes) from other systems?
- What would an ideal "specialist-ready" patient summary look like?
- How much pre-visit work do you delegate vs. do yourself?

## 4. Cross-Provider Coordination (10 min)

- Who do you most need to communicate with about a shared patient (PCP, other specialists, care managers)?
- What's broken about that communication today?
- How do you want to share your assessment and plan back to the care team?
- How do you handle co-management of chronic conditions across multiple specialists?
- How do you avoid stepping on another provider's plan (medication changes, conflicting advice)?

## 5. Procedures & Scheduling (8 min)

- Walk me through how a procedure gets scheduled today.
- What pre-op information needs to reach the patient, and how does that go wrong?
- How do you handle pre-authorization workflows?
- What post-procedure follow-up communication do patients need? How is it delivered?
- How should procedure-related messages route differently from general clinical messages?

## 6. Patient Communication & Education (7 min)

- For your specialty, what patient education matters most — and what's hardest to convey?
- How do you want patient-facing education to be personalized to their condition?
- What messaging workflows differ for your specialty (e.g., post-op questions, symptom check-ins)?
- How do you want to handle patients who message you outside your scope of care?

## 7. Care Plans & Long-Term Cases (5 min)

- For long-arc patients, how do you track plan progression today?
- What would a shared care plan need to be genuinely useful (not just another document)?
- How do you want patients to participate in their care plan?

## 8. Reliability, Performance & Workflow Fit (3 min)

- What workflow assumptions get broken when you're in a procedure room or between cases?
- What mobile or tablet use cases matter for you specifically?
- What's your tolerance for downtime on procedure days?

## 9. Data & Outcomes (2 min)

- What outcomes data do you wish you had visibility into for your patients?
- What metrics matter for your specialty that a general portal might overlook?

## 10. Wrap-Up & Vision (2 min)

- If referrals and coordination "just worked," what would change about your week?
- What would make this portal feel built for specialists, not just retrofit from a PCP design?
- Anything important I didn't ask?
