# Interview Questions: Tech-Limited Senior (Patient, 70+)

**Persona Snapshot:** Wants to stay independent and informed but finds many digital tools confusing. Values simplicity, large text, clear language, and trustworthy human backup when things go wrong.

**Session Length:** ~60 minutes
**Format:** Slower pace, plenty of pauses. Avoid jargon. Offer to demo concretely rather than describe abstractly. Allow a family member to sit in if the participant prefers.

---

## 1. Warm-Up & Comfort (5 min)

- Tell me a little about your day-to-day — do you live alone, with family, or near loved ones?
- What kinds of health concerns are on your mind these days?
- How do you usually contact your doctor's office today?
- Do you use a smartphone, tablet, or computer? Which feels most comfortable?

## 2. Current Experience with Technology (10 min)

- What's something on your phone or computer that you *do* feel comfortable using? Why does it work for you?
- Tell me about a time technology frustrated you recently. What happened?
- When something on a screen goes wrong, what do you do? Who do you ask?
- Have you ever tried the current patient portal? What was that like?
- What words or buttons on screens have confused you (e.g., "submit," "portal," "MFA")?

## 3. Connecting with the Doctor's Office (10 min)

- When you need to talk to your doctor's office, what's the easiest way for you today?
- How do you feel about leaving a message vs. talking to a real person?
- If you sent a question through an app, how would you want to know it was received?
- How long is "too long" to wait for a reply?
- Would you want a phone call back, a text, or something else?

## 4. Appointments (7 min)

- How do you keep track of upcoming appointments today (calendar, paper, family member)?
- What information helps you feel ready for a visit — directions, what to bring, what to expect?
- Would you want reminders? How many, and how (call, text, email)?
- Has transportation or finding the office ever been a problem? What helped?

## 5. Prescriptions (7 min)

- Walk me through how you get your medications refilled today.
- How do you remember to take your medications?
- Has there ever been a mix-up with a prescription? What happened?
- Would a simple list of your current medications be helpful? In what format?

## 6. Understanding Health Information (8 min)

- When you get test results, how do you usually find out what they mean?
- What kinds of words from doctors do you wish were explained more clearly?
- Would you want to read your visit summary afterwards? How detailed?
- Is text size, contrast, or screen brightness ever a problem for you?

## 7. Trust, Safety & Help (8 min)

- How do you feel about your health information being on a phone or computer?
- Have you ever worried about scams or someone pretending to be your doctor's office?
- Who do you trust to help you with technology — and would you want them to help here too?
- Would you want a family member to be able to see your records or help schedule visits?
- If something in the app didn't work, what would make you feel okay asking for help?

## 8. Simplicity & Preferences (3 min)

- If we showed you only three things on the main screen, what would you want them to be?
- Do you prefer to read, listen, or watch when learning something new?
- What would make you feel proud of yourself for using something like this?

## 9. Wrap-Up (2 min)

- What's one thing that would make a tool like this feel "made for me"?
- Is there anything you wanted to say that I didn't ask about?
