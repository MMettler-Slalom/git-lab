# Interview Questions: Chronic Condition Manager (Patient, 45–65)

**Persona Snapshot:** Manages one or more ongoing conditions (e.g., diabetes, hypertension). Coordinates with multiple specialists, tracks medications and lab trends, and wants a complete picture of their health over time.

**Session Length:** ~60 minutes
**Format:** Semi-structured; open-ended follow-ups encouraged.

---

## 1. Warm-Up & Background (5 min)

- Can you walk me through your health situation at a high level — what conditions are you actively managing?
- How many providers or specialists are typically involved in your care in a given year?
- How comfortable are you with technology in general (smartphone, banking apps, etc.)?
- What devices do you most often use to interact with healthcare services (phone, tablet, laptop)?

## 2. Current Experience & Pain Points (10 min)

- Walk me through the last time you needed something from your healthcare provider — what did you do first?
- What's the most frustrating part of managing your health today?
- Where do you currently feel like information about you "falls through the cracks" between providers?
- Have you ever given up on using the patient portal? What made you stop?
- What workarounds have you built (sticky notes, spreadsheets, paper folders) to compensate for tools that don't work well?

## 3. Care Team Communication (10 min)

- When you have a non-urgent clinical question, how do you currently get an answer? How long does it usually take?
- What would a "reasonable" response time from your care team feel like?
- Who on your care team do you wish you could message that you currently can't?
- How do you want to be notified when a provider responds — push, email, text, phone call?
- Have you ever felt a message you sent was misunderstood or got lost? What happened?

## 4. Lab Results & Health Information (10 min)

- When new lab results come back, how do you prefer to learn about them — before, with, or after your provider's interpretation?
- How useful are the explanations you currently get with results? What's missing?
- Do you ever try to compare results over time? How do you do that today?
- What would help you understand whether a result is "good," "bad," or "watch this"?
- How much detail do you actually want to see vs. a simplified summary?

## 5. Medications & Adherence (8 min)

- Walk me through how you keep track of what medications you're on and when to take them.
- How do you handle refills today? Where does that process break down?
- Would reminders be helpful, annoying, or both? What would make them useful vs. nagging?
- How do you handle medication questions or side effects between appointments?
- Do you use any apps or devices (pill organizers, smartwatches, glucose monitors) you'd want connected?

## 6. Appointments & Scheduling (5 min)

- How do you usually schedule a visit — phone, online, in person? Why?
- What information do you need to see before you can confidently book an appointment?
- How do you coordinate appointments across multiple specialists?
- What would make rescheduling or cancelling easier?

## 7. Health Tracking & Trends (5 min)

- Do you log any health data at home today (BP, glucose, weight)? How?
- Would you want that data visible to your providers? Under what conditions?
- What trends or patterns would you most want the portal to surface for you?

## 8. Trust, Privacy & Accessibility (4 min)

- How do you feel about your health information being stored and shared digitally?
- Are there things you would *not* want visible in a portal (mental health notes, certain diagnoses)?
- Are there accessibility needs we should know about (vision, dexterity, language)?
- Who else (spouse, adult child, caregiver) should be able to see or act on your behalf?

## 9. Wrap-Up & Vision (3 min)

- If you could wave a magic wand and change one thing about managing your health digitally, what would it be?
- What would make you go from occasional user to daily user of a portal like this?
- Is there anything important about your experience that I didn't ask about?
